# Google Play listing: English

## Title

Password Generator

## Short description

Create passwords offline. No ads, accounts or tracking. Passwords stay local.

## Full description

Create passwords directly on your Android device with Password Generator. The app works offline, without advertising, accounts, analytics or tracking.

Choose the rules you need:
• Password length from 4 to 64 characters.
• Lowercase and uppercase letters, numbers and symbols.
• At least one character from every enabled character group.
• Exclude visually similar characters.
• Exclude duplicate characters. If the selected set is too small, the app explains the problem instead of silently changing your rules.
• Quick length presets: 16, 24 and 32 characters.
• A password-strength indicator based on length, character variety and common weak patterns. The score is a guide, not a guarantee of security.

Privacy by design:
• Passwords are generated locally with Android's cryptographic random source, SecureRandom.
• Generated passwords are never saved by the app. Only your generator settings are stored.
• No INTERNET permission, remote servers or cloud synchronization.
• Android application backup is disabled.
• Passwords start hidden. Revealing a password enables Android's screen-capture protection.
• Copied passwords are marked as sensitive. The app attempts to remove its own clipboard value after 60 seconds when Android permits access, without replacing newer clipboard content. Other apps and the operating system may have their own clipboard behavior.

The interface supports English and Russian, system light and dark themes, and Dynamic Color on Android 12 and later.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. No registration, payment or special access is needed. The vibration permission is used for haptic feedback.

Project website and privacy policy are available from About. External links open in your browser.
